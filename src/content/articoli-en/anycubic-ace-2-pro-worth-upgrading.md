Anycubic opened a **trade-in** programme for owners of a first-generation ACE Pro, to move up to the ACE 2 Pro. The question is whether the generational jump justifies it.

## The programme in brief

| | |
|---|---|
| Window | 27 February &ndash; 27 March 2026 |
| Availability | limited to 5,000 units |
| Requirements | ACE Pro SN code + order number |

The unit cap and the narrow window are the two things pushing you toward a fast decision — worth being conscious of, because haste is the worst adviser on a purchase that isn't essential.

## What actually changes

- **Four independent brushless motors.** The technical reason everything else works better: each channel has its own motor, so movement is no longer tied to a shared mechanism.
- **Filament change speed doubled.** On prints with many colour changes, change time is time the machine isn't depositing material. Halving it is noticeable on long jobs.
- **Drying up to 65 &deg;C, including during printing.** This is the most substantial addition. Being able to dry filament while you print, rather than only beforehand, changes the result on PETG, TPU, PA and generally anything that absorbs moisture.
- **Active humidity control and sealed storage.** Damp filament produces uneven extrusion, dull surfaces and brittleness. Sealed storage with active control is effectively a permanent dry box.
- **Filament runout detection and automatic refill.** It removes the print lost because the spool ended at three in the morning.

Stated compatibility: Kobra X, Kobra S1, Kobra 3 and the Max series.

## Is it worth it?

There's only one deciding factor: **how much filament moisture actually bothers you.**

If you print almost exclusively PLA in a dry room, much of what you're paying for will go unused: PLA is forgiving, and faster changes alone don't justify an upgrade.

If you regularly work with PETG, TPU, nylon or filled materials — or if you live somewhere humid and have already binned prints because of it — drying during printing and sealed storage solve a problem you genuinely have.
